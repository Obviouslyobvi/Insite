## Milestone 5: developer-facing website features

Delivered as working, live pages on the program site build. The developer page presents the program start to finish in six steps drawn from the Developer Guide, carries the three program summary sentences and the plain-language statement of limits, and routes visitors to the fee estimator and the application. The application page implements the INS-201 pre-screening intake as an interactive form: the visitor enters project basics, checks off the six data package items, and the page generates a submission summary and a pre-addressed email, entirely in the visitor's browser, with nothing stored or transmitted by the page itself. Navigation is wired across the site. Live links: https://raw.githack.com/Obviouslyobvi/Insite/master/05_WEBSITE/developers.html and https://raw.githack.com/Obviouslyobvi/Insite/master/05_WEBSITE/developer_application.html. Dated file copies accompany this memorandum as archive records; the live links are the working deliverable, and the pages move to insite-ca.org when hosting access is provided.

## Milestone 6: capital markets website features

Delivered as a working, live investor page presenting the security stack as revised per the founder's July 20 markup, including the tax-status point, with the Read-this-first statement and direct routes to the Tax-Equivalent Yield Calculator, the Capital Partner Brief, and the Investor Guide. Live link: https://raw.githack.com/Obviouslyobvi/Insite/master/05_WEBSITE/investors.html. One element of the milestone remains open by its nature: the secure portal, which awaits the founder's approval of the public-versus-portal split already marked chapter by chapter inside the Investor Guide. The portal build proceeds on that approval.

## Milestone 2 completion: the populated fee database

The pilot dataset is delivered: thirteen development impact and capacity fees for the City of Sacramento, drawn from the city's official Fees and Charges open data service, which is the city's posting under Government Code 65940.1. Every row carries its own queryable source reference. Dollar amounts are recorded where the city states them; where the city publishes the schedule as an attachment, the row says so and names the attachment rather than guessing a number. The School Impact Fees row is included as inventory and marked excluded from INSITE financing at launch. With this delivery, the only open Milestone 2 item is the migration into Airtable, which waits on creation of the HGF Airtable account.
