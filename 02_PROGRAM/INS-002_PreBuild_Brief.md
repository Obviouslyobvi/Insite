5. **Consultant seats are labeled "selection in progress."** No firm names appear until Dennis picks them.

- The market size shows its arithmetic and its $25,000-per-unit assumption in plain view.
- SCIP and BOLD are named and credited. The paper claims a differentiated operating model, never a first-ever mechanism.
- The paper is marked pre-launch draft, not an offer of financing, and subject to bond counsel review before any external use.
