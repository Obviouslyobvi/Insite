# INS-303: Investor Guide, Outline and Content Split
**Version 0.1, July 12, 2026. The chapter plan for the capital-markets manual, built from the founder's July 12 notes, with each chapter mapped to its existing source material and designated PUBLIC (open website) or PORTAL (secure, credentialed). The founder's notes correctly observe that this outline decides the public-versus-portal split; here is that decision, chapter by chapter, ready for his edits.**

## The pipeline in one sentence
Origination writes privately placed, tax-secured notes one project at a time; a revolving warehouse credit facility carries the growing portfolio; and pooled tax-exempt bonds provide the optional permanent take-out once the portfolio stabilizes. The program's existing documents describe the origination layer; this manual adds the layers above it.
