## How to submit

Provide each document as a separate PDF named by its item number and the project name (example: E.1_Preliminary-Title-Report_Oakcrest.pdf). Submit the package complete; the Program Administrator confirms completeness before any appraisal assignment is issued, and incomplete packages are returned with the missing items identified. Any material change after submission must be disclosed promptly to the Program Administrator and the appraiser. Items marked "if applicable" or "if available" may be omitted with a brief note in the Comments column.

## Eligibility snapshot

Residential subdivisions of 2 to 50 lots in participating California jurisdictions. Financing supports eligible development impact fees and public improvement obligations. Program underwriting requires appraised value of at least four times the proposed financing. Minimum financing size is determined during feasibility screening. School fees are excluded from program financing at launch; the school fee schedule is still collected below because it is part of the total tax-burden analysis.

## Why each section exists

This package is the foundation of the appraisal (performed under the program's appraisal manual, INS-101) and of underwriting. Sections E.1 through E.3 establish what the property is and what has been approved; E.4 and E.5 establish what it costs to finish and what the public charges are; E.6 and E.7 establish what could restrict development; E.8 and E.9 support the value analysis; E.10 is the developer's certification.
