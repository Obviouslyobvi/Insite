FOR BANKS AND PRIVATE CAPITAL  ·  PRELIMINARY, PRE-LAUNCH

Small notes. County-collected. Four-to-one covered.

INSITE originates privately placed, tax-secured notes on small residential subdivisions, one note per project, serviced through the county property tax roll. The program is pre-launch and this page says exactly what is proven and what is not.

The security stack

Recorded special-tax lien, billed and collected with the property tax bill, foreclosure remedy behind it
Four-to-one appraised cushion by an independent MAI to State Treasurer (CDIAC) standards; the statute requires three
Zone isolation: each note is secured only by its own project's special taxes (Gov. Code 53339.3(d)); no cross-collateralization
Rate stability for payers: an occupied home's tax cannot rise more than 10 percent for others' delinquency (53321(d))

Servicing

Annual levy on the county roll, collections reconciliation, delinquency tracking, and investor reporting run by the program administrator with an established tax-roll administration firm engaged first.

Pipeline logic

62,372 units permitted in 1-to-4-unit structures in California in 2025 (Census primary data, recomputed)
At an assumed $25,000 of financeable fees per unit, roughly $1.56 billion per year of fee obligations in the measured segment (assumption stated)
SB 684 and SB 1123 now compel 60-day ministerial approval of ten-lot subdivisions, growing exactly this segment
Read this first. No note has closed. No purchaser is committed. Pricing is set at term sheet, not in marketing. Per-project execution cost is a founder estimate of $5,000 to $30,000 pending quotes. The pilot is deliberately small and designed to prove per-deal economics and purchaser appetite, the two ways this fails.
