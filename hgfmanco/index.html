When a loan stops performing

Rarely a simple story.

It is the sixteenth. The grace period ended yesterday. You are at the kitchen table with the servicer’s statement open, and the payment column on one of your notes reads zero for the second month. The borrower’s phone goes to voicemail. Your shoulders have crept up toward your ears, and you realize you have read the same line four times.

Loans that miss are seldom clean. There is a bankruptcy filing, a title cloud, a junior lien, a borrower who wants to pay and cannot, or a property that needs work before it can carry itself. A standard playbook stalls here. Every asset and program has its own story, and rarely a simple one.

Debt portfolios

Two books. Two disciplines.

Performing debt is bought for steady, monthly cash flow at conservative leverage. Distressed debt is bought at a discount and resolved event by event. The firm runs both.

Performing debt

Overlooked because it is small, structured, or inefficiently traded.

HGF specializes in acquiring and managing performing debt opportunities that are often overlooked because of complexity, specialized structures, smaller transaction sizes, or inefficient market segments.

Distressed debt

Bought for the probability of recovery, not the headline yield.

HGF invests in distressed mortgages, focusing on opportunities with a high probability of delivering above-average returns while minimizing the risk. Resolution comes through loan workouts, restructurings, and asset resolution strategies.

How we work

Clear thinking. Smart solutions. Better outcomes.

Clear thinking

We look beyond the numbers to understand the full picture: the borrower, the collateral, the liens, the court calendar, and the exit.

The team spans loan servicing, judicial and non-judicial foreclosure, bankruptcy, forensic underwriting, creditor’s rights, and real estate redevelopment.

Smart solutions

Practical strategies for assets and programs that require more than a standard playbook: workouts, restructurings, and asset resolution matched to each loan’s facts.

Borrowers get personalized guidance through every stage of a loan. A loan brought current is often the cleanest resolution there is.
