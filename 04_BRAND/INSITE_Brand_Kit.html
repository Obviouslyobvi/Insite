Typography

Headings and interface: Helvetica / Arial, bold, letter-spaced caps for bands.
Long-form body: Georgia, 10.5 to 11 pt, 1.6 line height (whitepaper, primer, memos).
Forms, tables, one-pagers: Helvetica / Arial 9 to 10 pt.

Voice

Values: Integrity, Transparency, Simplicity, Innovation, Reliability, Partnership.
Personality: professional, institutional, trusted, efficient, modern, technical, approachable.
Three writing rules: every number carries a source; every estimate is labeled an estimate; competitors are credited by name.

Logo system

The mark: a 4-by-4 parcel grid; the green staircase cluster reads as parcels developing over time. Green cells (row, column): 2-4, 3-3, 3-4, 4-2, 4-3.
Clear space: one grid cell on all sides. Minimum width: 140 px on screen, 1.25 inches in print.
Never: recolor, stretch, rotate, outline, add effects, or set the wordmark in another face.
Retired: the legacy header with the "School Fee" tagline (Header_2.png) is superseded pending the school-fee decision.
