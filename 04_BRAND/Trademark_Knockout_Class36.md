# Trademark Knockout Screen: Class 36
**Run July 11, 2026, at the builder's direction. Method: the USPTO search interface could not be driven directly this session, so candidate marks were located through indexed USPTO records (Justia, uspto.report) and every decisive fact below was then verified against USPTO's own Trademark Status and Document Retrieval system. Each row carries its source.**

## The operating rule, as supplied
"Anything live, in Class 36, covering financing or financial administration, with the same sound, is a serious conflict. Stop and take Option A."

## Decisive findings

**INSITE, serial 98837494.** Status: LIVE, application published for opposition (past examination, on the path to registration). International Class 036. Owner: Security Administrative Services, LLC. Filed November 5, 2024. Services per the application: administration of insurance services for others, including third-party administration of insurance claims, premiums, and benefit plans, and electronic processing of insurance claims and payment data. Sources: tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn98837494; trademarks.justia.com/988/37/insite-98837494.html. **This is the exact name, live, in Class 36, covering financial administration.**

**INSIGHT CAPITAL SOLUTIONS, serial 99761274.** Status: LIVE, application awaiting examination. International Class 036, class status ACTIVE. Owner: Insight Capital Solutions, LLC. Filed April 13, 2026. Services: "Financing services." Sources: tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn99761274; uspto.report/TM/99761274. **INSIGHT is the phonetic twin of INSITE, live, in Class 36, covering financing.**

## Neighboring classes, for completeness

**INSIGHT CAPITAL, serial 98211413.** LIVE, registered and active. Class 035. Owner: Insight Capital, LLC. Services: business management of insurance agencies and brokers on an outsourcing basis. Sources: tsdr.uspto.gov/statusview/sn98211413; uspto.report/TM/98211413.

**INSITE, registration 6610415.** LIVE, registered. Class 042 (software as a service for e-commerce). Owner lineage: Insite Software Solutions / Optimizely. Already on file as claims register entry F4; not a financial-services mark.

An older multi-class record (MY INSITE, serial 75935827, filed 2000) returned no parseable current status and was set aside rather than guessed at.

## Verdict under the rule
The rule is tripped twice: once by the exact name and once by the homophone, both live in Class 36, one covering financial administration and the other financing outright. Per the rule as written: stop, and take Option A.
