What a tax-exempt yield is worth in taxable terms.

Interest on qualifying municipal securities is exempt from federal income tax, and for California residents holding California issues, from state income tax as well. This tool converts a tax-exempt yield into the taxable yield an investor would need to match it after tax, using the additive combined-rate convention common to major fund calculators.

Method: combined rate = federal + state (when the state exemption applies) + net investment income tax (when selected); tax-equivalent yield = tax-exempt yield ÷ (1 − combined rate). This is the simplified additive convention; individual circumstances (deduction limits, AMT, phase-outs) change real results.
